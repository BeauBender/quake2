# FIX176: check-gate-parallel's tty case names the process that stopped (slice 1)

## What happened

On 2026-11-21 a merge gate went red on the `tty` case of `tests/check-gate-parallel.sh` with
"the run stopped on a terminal with tostop (test.sh pid N), and was ended by pid". FIX174 made
the case read the state of every process descending from `test.sh`, and one was in a `T` state, but the
line named only test.sh's pid. Nothing said which process wrote to the terminal from a background group.

## Slice 1: the message

On a stop, `c_tty` reads, before it ends anything, for each process in a `T` state: pid, `stat`, `pgid`,
`comm` and `args`. The args lose `$HOME` (printed as `~`) and are cut to 120 characters. The stop line
is `tty: the run stopped on a terminal with tostop (test.sh pid N), and was ended by pid; stopped:
[pid=.. stat=T.. pgid=.. comm=.. args=..]`. The other processes' `pid=stat` go on one `tty: state:` line
below it, cut to 200 characters. The slow-run line is unchanged.

In the `ttymsg` mutant (the scheduler without its SIGTTOU ignore) the stopped process is the scheduler's
shell, the one that runs `gate-checks.sh` under `test.sh`; the case pins that its `comm` ends in `sh`.

## Mutants (smut lines in the self-test, case `ttymsg`)

`tty-no-pgid`, `tty-no-comm`, `tty-no-args`, `tty-no-stat` (a field dropped), `tty-no-state-line`,
`tty-others-in-stop` (the others' states in the stop line), `tty-home-kept` (`$HOME` left in the args),
`tty-no-cut` (no 120 cut), `tty-read-after-kill` (the read moved after the KILL).

## Left for slice 2

Find which process writes to the terminal without ignoring `SIGTTOU` under gate load, and fix that
writer. `scripts/test.sh`, `scripts/gate-checks.sh` and the signal dispositions are untouched here.
