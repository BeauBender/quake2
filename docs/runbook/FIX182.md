# FIX182: case (f) of the image-decoder self-test ignores a shell's "Killed: 9" job notice

## What happened

Case (f) of `tests/check-image-decoder-selftest.sh` runs a probe around the check's `reap` function and
compared the probe's whole output, stdout and stderr together, with its four result lines. When `reap`
SIGKILLs a background run, bash may print its own job notice (`line N: PID Killed: 9 ...`) before `wait`
collects the job. That depends on timing, so a correct reap failed the case now and then (CI on #676).

## What changed

- The probe's stdout (the four result lines) and stderr go to separate files, `<name>.out` and
  `<name>.err`. Only the stdout is compared; on a failure both are printed.
- Case (f) is now the function `reapcase <name> <reap file> <force notice>`, run four times: on the
  check's own `reap`, with a `Killed: 9` line forced onto the probe's stderr (`FORCE_NOTICE=1`), and on
  two mutants of `reap`.

## Red on mutants

- `reap` kills every child of the run, not only the named program (`other child: killed`): case (f) red.
- `reap` spares the named child (`named child: alive`): case (f) red.
- The forced notice: green with this change, red on the previous comparison (stderr merged into the
  compared output).
