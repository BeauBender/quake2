# FIX139: check-metalfx-report's lock assertions failed on the CI runner

On the macos-latest runner (run 44549989930) `tests/check-metalfx-report.sh` failed with
`e2e-lock-wait` (5 times) and `e2e-lock-off` (3 times), and the mutants `stop-unheld` and
`stop-wait-outlives-lock` "survived" because the real-code case had already failed.

## Cause

It was not a timing assumption. The runner exports `CI=true` and `GITHUB_ACTIONS=true`, and
`scripts/metalfx-report/mfx.py` turns the host lock off when it sees both (the lock is for the
build host, a free runner has none). The lock cases built their environment with `env -u BENCH_LOCK*`
but left those two variables in, so on the runner:

- `e2e-lock-wait` ran the generator with the lock already off: it did not wait for the holder,
  noted no holder, and printed "the host lock is off" (the five failed assertions);
- `e2e-lock-off` expects the lock to be off only for the one reason it names and on for the final
  case with no reason (exit 3 and no such line); with the variables inherited, the BENCH_LOCK=0
  reason was masked and the last case was refused as off (the three failed assertions);
- the sleeps, polls and bounds in those cases (`sleep 10` holder, `BENCH_LOCK_WAIT=60`,
  `BENCH_LOCK_POLL=0.2`, the 5 s and 20 s ceilings) already wait on files and pids or are
  ceilings; they were not the cause.

## Fix

The three `lenv` definitions of the lock group in `tests/check-metalfx-report.sh` now also
`env -u CI -u GITHUB_ACTIONS`. The one case that tests the CI reason sets both itself. Nothing a
case proves was weakened. `check-metalfx-report.sh` moves from `tests/ci-local-only.txt` to
`tests/ci-checks.txt`.

## Proof

The lock group of the old script under `CI=true GITHUB_ACTIONS=true` fails as the runner did; the
fixed script passes in full under the same variables, and its lock group passes under
`nice -n 20` with both mutants refused (durations in the card's report).
